QUANTIX Normalization Philosophy
Normalization is the core mechanism that allows QUANTIX to compare products, systems, and creative works that come from different industries, different scales, and different measurement units.

QUANTIX does not use absolute values.
It uses relative positioning within each comparison group to ensure fairness, transparency, and cross‑domain consistency.

🔹 1. Relative Scoring, Not Absolute Scoring
QUANTIX evaluates each metric relative to the best and worst performers in the group.

This ensures:

no domain is punished for having naturally smaller or larger numbers

no industry gains an advantage from inflated metrics

every comparison adapts to its own context

This is why QUANTIX can compare:

smartphones

TVs

cameras

video‑games

music

markets

scientific domains

…using the same five axes.

🔹 2. Domain‑Adjusted Ranges
Different industries have different natural ranges.

Examples:

Smartphone battery life ranges from 4–12 hours

TV brightness ranges from 300–3000 nits

Camera ISO performance ranges from 100–102,400

Music clarity has no numerical unit at all

QUANTIX normalizes each metric within its domain, not across domains.

This keeps scoring meaningful and prevents distortion.

🔹 4. Weighted Interpretation (Not Weighted Math)
QUANTIX does not apply numerical weights.
Instead, each axis has a conceptual weight depending on the domain.

Examples:

In smartphones, Efficiency (battery + performance per watt) is critical

In TVs, Purity (color accuracy + noise control) is dominant

In cameras, Robustness (weather sealing + durability) matters more

In music, Purity (clarity + tonal accuracy) is central

This keeps QUANTIX flexible without adding hidden math.

🔹 5. Noise Reduction Through Grouping
QUANTIX avoids comparing unrelated products.

Examples:

Flagship phones are compared with flagships

Budget phones with budget phones

Mirrorless cameras with mirrorless cameras

OLED TVs with OLED TVs

This reduces noise and increases fairness.

🔹 6. Qualitative → Quantitative Mapping
Some metrics are not numerical (e.g., “ecosystem maturity,” “gameplay stability,” “emotional impact”).

QUANTIX maps these into consistent qualitative tiers, then normalizes them.

This allows:

subjective metrics to be treated consistently

creative domains (music, games) to be evaluated alongside technical ones

🔹 7. Cross‑Domain Alignment
After normalization, all axes share the same 0–10 scale.

This allows QUANTIX to:

compare different products

compare different industries

compare different years

compare different technologies

…without losing meaning.

🧠 Why Normalization Matters
Normalization is what makes QUANTIX:

fair

transparent

domain‑aware

scientifically inspired

cross‑industry compatible

It ensures that QUANTIX is not just a scoring system —
it is a universal comparison framework.
