# QUANTIX-Framework
What is QUANTIX?

QUANTIX uses the procedures that determine the composition and structure of matter to determine the composition and structure of data.

QUANTIX provides a consistent lens for comparing anything — from smartphones and TVs to cameras, video‑games, music, and even scientific or market‑based domains.

The Five QUANTIX Axes
Each axis represents a universal dimension of performance or quality:

Consistency — stability, predictability, reliability

Purity — clarity, cleanliness, accuracy

Efficiency — output per unit input

Robustness — resilience under stress

Scalability — long‑term growth potential

These axes remain constant across all domains.
Only their interpretation changes depending on the product or field.

Why QUANTIX Exists
Modern products and systems are increasingly complex.
Traditional reviews often mix subjective impressions with inconsistent criteria.

QUANTIX solves this by offering:

a unified structure

a scientific mindset

a cross‑domain vocabulary

a visual comparison method (radar charts)

a normalization philosophy that keeps evaluations fair

It is not a rating system.
It is a thinking framework.

Inspired by Analytical Chemistry
Analytical Chemistry breaks down substances into measurable components.
QUANTIX applies the same philosophy to products and systems:

define the axes

normalize the data

compare across categories

visualize the results

This makes QUANTIX both scientific and flexible.

Domain‑Specific Interpretations
QUANTIX adapts to each field through equivalence tables.

Examples:

Smartphones
Consistency → Software stability

Purity → Display & camera cleanliness

Efficiency → Battery & performance efficiency

Robustness → Thermal & build resilience

Scalability → Ecosystem growth

TVs
Consistency → Panel stability

Purity → Image cleanliness

Efficiency → Energy & brightness efficiency

Robustness → Burn‑in resistance & HDR stability

Scalability → OS ecosystem

Cameras
Consistency → Autofocus reliability

Purity → Optical & sensor clarity

Efficiency → Battery & processing efficiency

Robustness → Weather sealing & durability

Scalability → Lens ecosystem

More equivalence tables are available in the /docs folder.

Normalization Philosophy
QUANTIX uses a relative normalization approach, ensuring that comparisons remain fair across:

different product categories

different generations

different technologies

Normalization is conceptual and domain‑agnostic.
The internal scoring engine is not part of this repository.

Examples and Radar Charts
The /charts folder contains example QUANTIX radar charts for:

Smartphones

TVs

Cameras

Video‑games

Music

These charts illustrate how QUANTIX can be applied visually.
They use representative shapes, not raw scoring data.

How to Use QUANTIX
You can apply QUANTIX to any domain by:

Defining how each axis translates to your field

Normalizing your criteria

Scoring each item internally

Visualizing the results with a radar chart

Templates are available in the /examples folder.
